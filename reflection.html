Reflection

What it was like to build this site, one week at a time.

Weekly log

Week of Unit 6

Semantics

This was the first week and the hardest one to start. We had to decide what the website would look like before we even had all our notes, so we chose a simple design in wine red and decided to present each concept like a dictionary entry.

Entailment was the most difficult topic to explain, so instead of writing only definitions we added a small quiz with the examples from class. Making the quiz actually helped us study.

Week of Unit 7

Pragmatics

Pragmatics was shorter, but it was harder to show on a web page because it's all about context. We decided to use the same sentence in different situations so the reader can see how the meaning changes.

Watching the video and then explaining it in our own words made us realize how much we depend on context in Spanish without thinking about it.

Language variation

Sociolinguistics

This unit had a lot of content, so organizing it was the main challenge. We split it into smaller sections (varieties, styles, levels, and identity) and added Costa Rican examples, because they made the ideas easier for us to understand.

It was fun to realize that things we say every day, like mae or pajilla, are real examples of what we study in class.

Group projects

Presentations

We presented these topics in class in groups, so we already knew the content well. Still, adding them to the website was more work than we expected: in class we could explain each slide out loud, but on a web page the text has to explain the idea by itself to someone who wasn't there.

Going through the slides again after presenting also helped us find a few mistakes we hadn't noticed before, which was a good review for the exam.

Portfolio and research report

Putting it all together

The portfolio was the biggest project of the semester. Analyzing the same source through nine different areas showed us how connected they are: one short line like “This is fine” has grammar, sound, meaning, and context all working at the same time.

Writing the research report also changed how we saw our first presentation. We realized that colour and color are just spelling, and that the real morphological differences are in forms like learnt and learned or got and gotten.

Final reflection

Building this website week by week was different from any assignment we've done before. At the beginning we thought it would be mostly about design, but we ended up spending more time deciding how to explain each topic clearly.

Writing our own notes and examples forced us to really understand the concepts instead of just memorizing definitions, and using examples from Costa Rican Spanish made the theory feel much closer to our everyday life.

If we did it again, we would add content right after each class instead of leaving several topics for the same week, and we would include more of our own images and audio. We're proud of the final result, and we think it will be a useful study guide for the exam.
